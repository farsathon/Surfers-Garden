## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the relaxed elegance of Arugam Bay, Sri Lanka—translating the spirit of "Surf. Relax. Repeat." into an elevated, tactile digital sanctuary. It marries the sun-bleached natural warmth of tropical surf culture with high-end hospitality precision. The target audience comprises discerning global nomads, surf purists seeking luxury, and design-conscious travelers who value architectural simplicity, craft, and restorative experiences.

The design movement is **Organic Editorial Minimalism** punctuated with subtle **tactile and warm glassmorphic** details. Interfaces prioritize serene spatial rhythm, sun-drenched photography, graceful typographic proportions, and earthy, natural textures. Visual noise is stripped away to induce an immediate sensory deceleration—mirroring the feeling of warm teak underfoot, salty afternoon air, and quiet coastal sunsets.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural coastal ecosystem of Arugam Bay:
- **Primary (`#1B4D4F` - Deep Ocean Teal)**: Evokes the deep Indian Ocean rollers off the point break. Used for authoritative typography, primary navigation items, and high-impact hero accents.
- **Secondary (`#D19A4B` - Sunlit Dune Gold)**: Reflects warm sunlit sand dunes and golden hour surf sessions. Deployed for booking CTAs, interactive highlights, and star amenities.
- **Tertiary (`#2B4736` - Palm Canopy Green)**: Inspired by the coastal jungle palms and lush garden retreats surrounding the bungalows. Used for secondary badges, environmental wellness sections, and organic borders.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` - Washed Raw Linen)**: The foundation canvas, providing a sun-bleached, glare-free warm alternative to harsh digital whites.
- **Named Colors**:
  - `terracotta-accent` (`#C45B38`): Natural Sri Lankan earthenware; accents special package alerts, active booking states, and seasonal offers.
  - `linen-white` (`#FDFCFA`): Pure elevated surface color for cards, modals, and input fields.
  - `sand-dune` (`#E8DFC8`): Muted sandy border strokes and subtle background alternation.
  - `deep-kelp` (`#112C26`): Deepest dark anchor for high-contrast footer backgrounds and ultra-dramatic typography.
  - `charcoal-palm` (`#1E2421`): High-legibility body copy tint, preventing the sterile harshness of pure black.

## Typography

The typography couples the editorial grace of **Noto Serif** with the breezy, geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Editorial Serifs (Noto Serif)**: Brings quiet prestige, heritage, and tropical literary charm to headlines, room titles, and hero narratives. Display titles feature gentle negative tracking to command serene presence.
- **Modern Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures effortless scannability across booking engines, room feature matrices, and body copy.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase using `label-lg` or `label-sm` with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`), evoking luxury editorial captions and boutique wayfinding.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on expansive breathing room, balanced asymmetry, and curated architectural grids.

- **Grid Architecture**: A 12-column responsive fluid grid with a maximum content container of 1360px.
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `gutter: 2rem`, canvas `margin: 4rem`. Generous vertical padding between sections (80px to 140px) mimics the open-air layout of boutique villas.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, canvas `margin: 2.5rem`.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, canvas `margin: 1.25rem`. Content effortlessly stacks into single-column editorial cards.
- **Rhythm**: Element padding and gaps follow a structured modular scale (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) that reinforces uncluttered contemplation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse sunlit layers rather than artificial harsh drop shadows.

- **Tonal Stacking**: Depth is achieved primarily through warm surface contrast. The background sits on `#F9F6F0` (Washed Raw Linen), with floating cards resting on `#FDFCFA` (Linen White) bordered with 1px hairline strokes of `#E8DFC8` (Sand Dune).
- **Ambient Sunlit Shadows**:
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(27, 77, 79, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 77, 79, 0.02)`.
  - *Floating Booking Bar / Modals*: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(17, 44, 38, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(17, 44, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Frosted Beach Glass**: Navigation bars, floating booking widgets, and photo overlays leverage `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` combined with `rgba(253, 252, 250, 0.82)` to preserve continuous connection with the ocean scenery below.

## Shapes

The design system implements a soft, refined border radius (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Standard interactive controls and cards use subtle 4px (`0.25rem`) to 8px (`0.5rem`) soft corners, mirroring clean architectural joinery, natural stone cuts, and custom teak millwork.
- Hero imagery and room suite feature frames feature 12px (`0.75rem`) radii to soften panoramic vistas without appearing juvenile or cartoonish.
- Pill forms (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for compact status tags, amenities chips, and secondary filter badges to evoke ocean-tumbled sea glass.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Actions
- **Primary CTA ("Reserve Suite")**: High-touch luxury finish using `#1B4D4F` background with `#FDFCFA` text. Padded at `1rem 2rem`, font token `label-lg`, subtle transition with gold hover bloom (`#D19A4B`).
- **Secondary CTA ("Explore Surf School")**: Transparent surface framed with a 1.5px border of `#1B4D4F`, text in `#1B4D4F`. On hover, fills smoothly with `#1B4D4F` and shifts text to `#FDFCFA`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Text-only with an animated 1px underline that expands from left to right on hover.

### Elevated Booking Bar
A signature floating architectural bar pinned across the base of the viewport on desktop:
- Background: Frosted linen glass (`rgba(253, 252, 250, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`).
- Border: 1px hairline `rgba(232, 223, 200, 0.8)`.
- Layout: 4 inline modules (Check-in / Check-out, Suite Class, Guests, Surf Concierge add-on) terminated by a solid `#D19A4B` (Golden Ochre) booking button.

### Room Suites Showcase & Editorial Cards
- **Card Base**: Surface `#FDFCFA`, hairline border `#E8DFC8`, soft corner radius (`0.5rem`).
- **Image Frame**: Generous 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio with gentle hover zoom (`scale(1.03)` over 600ms ease-out).
- **Content Block**: Accommodates Noto Serif headline, guest capacity metadata in tracked sans-serif caps, price anchor with daily rate breakdown, and a direct "View Sanctuary" text link.

### Amenities Badges & Chips
- Compact pill-shaped containers (`padding: 0.375rem 0.875rem`).
- Surface: `#F9F6F0` with text in `#2B4736` (Palm Canopy Green).
- Accompanied by minimalist 16px geometric line icons (e.g., custom surfboard racks, open-air yoga shala, organic breakfast, salt pool).

### Form Inputs & Contact Form
- **Inputs**: Crisp warm white fields (`#FDFCFA`) with 1px border `#E8DFC8`. Active focus triggers a 1px ring in `#1B4D4F` and soft amber tint.
- **Labels**: High-legibility `label-sm` positioned cleanly above each input with mandatory asterisks tinted in `terracotta-accent` (`#C45B38`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom custom-styled square/round controls with `#1B4D4F` fill and `#FDFCFA` checkmark glyphs upon selection.

### Immersive Photo Galleries & Marquee Accents
- Masonry and asymmetrical dual-column editorial galleries featuring warm natural light photography.
- Subtle marquee ribbon using the brand motto ("Surf. Relax. Repeat.") rendered in whisper-soft tone-on-tone serif typography (`#E8DFC8`) running across transition sections.
